Key Atlanta Roads — Driver Notes
I-75/85
Downtown Connector — Worst congestion in Atlanta. Avoid 7:30–9 AM southbound, 4:30–7 PM northbound. Use Peachtree St or Spring St as alternate.
I-285
The Perimeter — Encircles metro ATL. Western section (I-85 to I-75) clears 15–20 min faster than eastern. Use for suburban pickups.
GA-400
Northside — Sandy Springs, Buckhead to Midtown. Heavy southbound AM, northbound PM. Toll road — keep Peach Pass current.
I-20
East-West corridor — Connects airport area to east/west suburbs. Avoid peak hours near downtown interchange at I-285.
US-78
Decatur / Stone Mountain — Good alternative for east-side runs when I-20 is backed up. Less congested during rush.

City of Atlanta — Atlanta PD VFH Section
Atlanta Taxi Regulations
Chapter 162, Atlanta City Code · GA HB 225
License & Permit Requirements
01
For-Hire Endorsement — State of Georgia issues endorsements through DDS. Required on your CDL/driver's license. License plate starts with "TX" for taxis.
02
CPNC (Certificate of Public Necessity and Convenience) — Required to operate. Held by the company or individual owner. Annual renewal through Atlanta PD VFH Section.
03
Master Taxicab License — $1,500/year for a single cab. Required under GA Code § 40-2-168. Must be affiliated with a licensed company.
04
Georgia Residency — Must be GA resident for at least 1 year prior to application (Atlanta City Ordinance § 162-77).
05
Background Check — Fingerprinting by APD required. Cannot have 2+ Atlanta City Code violations in prior 2 years.
On-Road Rules — Must Know
06
Credit Cards Mandatory at ATL — All Vehicles for Hire at Hartsfield-Jackson MUST accept credit cards. No exceptions. Violation = impoundment.
07
Vehicle Age — Must generally be a model year within the last 16 years. Annual safety inspection required through Atlanta PD.
08
Cleanliness — Dirty cab at a taxi stand = correction sheet + forced to leave stand. VFH officers conduct spot inspections.
09
Written Receipts — Must be able to issue written receipts to passengers. English proficiency required to read traffic signs and obey law enforcement.
10
Sales Tax — Register with Georgia DOR to collect and remit sales tax if applicable to your service type. Consult a tax professional.
Airport Rules — Hartsfield-Jackson ATL
A1
Ground Transport Center — Taxi queue at Domestic Terminal west curb. International Terminal taxis at arrivals level, signed zone.
A2
Special Airport Permit — Drivers at ATL need Ground Transportation permits from the Airport's Ground Transportation Division, in addition to VFH permits.
A3
Flat Rate Zones — Posted flat rates apply to central Atlanta zones. For destinations outside zones, metered fare applies.
A4
Queue Discipline — Strict staging and queue rules enforced by VFH officers. Do not cut queue. Keep vehicle clean — officers conduct stand inspections.

Safety Tips — Atlanta
🛡
Always verify destination before starting. Trust your instincts — you can refuse unsafe situations.
📸
Note plate numbers of vehicles involved in disputes. Atlanta PD non-emergency line for non-critical incidents.
🌙
Late-night: keep doors locked until destination confirmed. Well-lit pickup/dropoff on Buckhead and downtown runs post-midnight.
💳
Non-payment: do not chase. Note passenger details and file report with ATL PD VFH Section and your company.
